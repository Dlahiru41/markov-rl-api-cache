\chapter{Experimental Results}

This chapter presents the experimental results of our Markov-RL based caching system.

\section{Experimental Setup}
We conducted a comprehensive evaluation consisting of:
\begin{itemize}
\item Total experiments: 3
\item Experiment categories: 2
\item Multiple random seeds for statistical significance
\end{itemize}

\section{Baseline Comparisons}
We compared our RL-based approach against standard caching baselines:
\begin{itemize}
\item LRU (Least Recently Used)
\item LFU (Least Frequently Used)
\item Random replacement
\item Static Markov policy
\end{itemize}

Figure~\ref{fig:baseline_comparison} shows the comparison results.

\section{Statistical Analysis}
All comparisons were tested for statistical significance using t-tests and ANOVA.
We report 95\% confidence intervals throughout.
